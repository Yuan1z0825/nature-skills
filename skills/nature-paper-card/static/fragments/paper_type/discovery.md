# Discovery / mechanism paper

Emphasize the question-to-evidence chain.

- Identify the observation, causal or mechanistic claim, intervention, controls, replication, and alternative explanations.
- Separate association, necessity, sufficiency, and mechanism.
- Map each figure to the narrowing of the proposed mechanism.
- Check biological or physical scale, model-system limits, temporal order, measurement validity, and independent replication.
- Do not infer causality from correlation or a mechanism from a phenotype alone.
- In Section 16, propose discriminating experiments between competing explanations.
